# Security

`csbridge` runs on your machine and logs in to the Claude Science app on it, so a flaw in it can
matter. If you find one, please report it privately: open the repository's **Security** tab and
choose **Report a vulnerability**. Please do not open a public issue for it.

Leave cookies, tokens and the content of your conversations out of the report. A description of
what happens and the steps that lead to it are what is needed.

Fixes go into the latest version on `main`.
